FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

What happens when beta ends?

Current beta orgs get 90 days to decide on Growth. If you pass, you keep everything you built — export your data anytime. If you subscribe, we grandfather beta pricing for the first year.

Is my data really isolated from other orgs?

Yes. Every table has an org_id column and a Postgres row-level-security policy enforcing it. Cross-org reads are impossible — not by trust, by database policy. See our architecture doc for the full details.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on any tier. Point your DNS at MemberForge, set your custom domain in admin, and the tenant router resolves it the same way it resolves the subdomain.

Do you charge for Stripe fees?

No. Stripe’s own processing fees apply (2.9% + $0.30), but MemberForge takes zero platform fee on top of that. Every dollar collected minus Stripe’s cut goes to your org.

What if my chapter has 500 members? 5,000?

No per-member fee. Unlimited members on every tier. If you’re past 2,500 and want dedicated support / SSO, that’s the Growth plan.
