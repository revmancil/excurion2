FAQ

Questions we get a lot.

Can I switch plans anytime?

Yes. Upgrade from your admin dashboard whenever you're getting close to your member limit — it takes effect immediately. Downgrade or cancel anytime from the same billing screen; you keep your current plan until the end of the billing period.

What happens if I hit my member limit?

You'll see it coming from your dashboard before it happens. Once you're at the limit, adding a new member prompts an upgrade — nothing about your existing site, members, or data is ever locked or degraded.

Is my data really isolated from other orgs?

Yes. Every table has an org_id column and a Postgres row-level-security policy enforcing it. Cross-org reads are impossible — not by trust, by database policy. See our architecture doc for the full details.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes, on every plan, including Free. Point your DNS at MemberForge, set your custom domain in admin, and the tenant router resolves it the same way it resolves the subdomain.

Do you take a cut of the dues and event fees I collect?

No. When your members pay dues, event fees, or buy from your store, Stripe's own processing fees apply (2.9% + $0.30), but MemberForge takes zero cut on top of that — every dollar minus Stripe's fee goes to your org. That's separate from your monthly plan cost, which only covers the platform itself.

What if my chapter has 500 members? 5,000?

No per-member fee within a plan — you pay the plan price, not a per-head charge. Free covers up to 50, Start up to 250, Growth up to 1,000, and Pro is unlimited for national orgs and federations.
