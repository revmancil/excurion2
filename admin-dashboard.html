Member Directory

0 records

Maintain your roster and bulk-upload records by CSV. Saving syncs matched records into the member portal directory (matched by email, then member number). Records without an email stay in this list only until an email is added.

CSV headers: member_number,first_name,middle_name,last_name,suffix,email,phone,status,joined_via,joined_at,birthday,lifetime_member_date,lifetime_member_number (minimum required: member_number). Re-uploading merges by member number — filled cells update existing rows, empty cells keep current values (no duplicates).
