## Full description

**Running a nonprofit is hard enough. Your books shouldn't be a mystery.**

If you've ever wondered why a $50,000 grant can't just go toward rent, what "net assets released from restrictions" means, or what actually goes on a Form 990, this guide is for you.

*The Nonprofit Accounting Handbook* explains nonprofit finance in plain English for the people who keep small and mid-sized organizations running:

- Executive directors who also handle the finances
- Board treasurers and finance committee members
- Bookkeepers coming over from the for-profit world
- Founders who want to set things up right from day one

No accounting degree required. Every technical term is explained when it first appears and collected in the glossary.

### What you'll learn

✔ **Donor restrictions, made simple.** Track restricted gifts so you never spend money the wrong way by accident.
✔ **A chart of accounts built for nonprofits.** A ready-to-use numbering system that lines up with the Form 990.
✔ **Recording revenue correctly.** Donations, pledges, foundation and government grants, in-kind gifts, and event tickets, with sample journal entries.
✔ **Program vs. overhead.** How to split expenses into program, management and fundraising, and how to allocate shared costs like rent and salaries.
✔ **The four financial statements.** Fully worked examples from a sample organization, line by line.
✔ **Budgeting and cash flow.** Build a board-ready budget, forecast cash, and set a reserve target.
✔ **Internal controls for small teams.** Protect your organization from fraud and email scams, even with only one or two staff.
✔ **Grant and federal award compliance.** Indirect cost rates, Uniform Guidance basics, and grant tracking.
✔ **IRS essentials.** Which Form 990 to file, donor acknowledgment rules, quid-pro-quo disclosures, non-cash gifts, and taxes on unrelated business income.
✔ **Audits and the board.** What your board is responsible for and how to prepare for your first audit.
